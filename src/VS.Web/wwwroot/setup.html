What VS stores

        

          VS stores normalized game and pitch records in PostgreSQL, not repeated copies of the full MLB live-feed JSON.
          Each pitch is protected from duplication by game ID plus MLB play ID.
        

        

          Historical scopes in GameCenter use this local database for 7-day, 30-day and season analytics.

L-Bar Advertising Media

      
Upload separate media for the left rail and bottom banner. Each position accepts PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, MP4, or WebM up to 250 MB. Videos play muted and loop automatically.

      

        Recommended media sizes
        Right rail: 840 × 1680 for 4K, 420 × 840 for 1080p, or 280 × 560 for 720p (1:2 ratio).
        Bottom banner: 3840 × 480 for 4K, 1920 × 240 for 1080p, or 1280 × 160 for 720p (approximately 8:1).
        Other dimensions are accepted, but media fills the slot and may be cropped.
        Media within 3% of the recommended aspect ratio is left unchanged. Other media is scaled and center-cropped automatically; the uploaded original is preserved.
